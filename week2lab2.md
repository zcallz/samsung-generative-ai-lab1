# Lab 2: Detailed Prompt Engineering Practice

Bu doküman, yapılandırılmış komut (prompt) mühendisliği döngüsünün Lab 2-1'den Lab 2-5'e kadar olan aşamalarındaki görevleri ve aktivite yansımalarını içermektedir. Odak noktamız, veri analitiği ve ileri düzey istatistik çalışmaları için gerçekçi bir "Yapay Zeka Çalışma Koçu" (AI Study Coach) tasarlamaktır.

## Lab 2-1: Step-by-Step Prompt Element Practice
Bu aşamada belirsiz bir komutun yüksek kaliteli bir çıktıya dönüşümünü deneyimledik.
* **Activity Reflection:** "I felt the biggest 'quality jump' at Step **[Context/Constraints]**, because **it forced the AI to stop giving generic advice and specifically tailor the plan to a rigorous statistics and data science workload**."
* "Also, The element I will use most often is **[Format Requirements]** because **I need the study schedules and code snippets presented in clean, easy-to-read markdown tables**."

## Lab 2-2: Creating My Own AI Study Coach
Bu adımda, planda kalmayı (adherence) ve gerçekçiliği merkeze alan bir çalışma koçu senaryosu tasarlandı.
* **Activity Reflection:** "I think adding a **[Buffer Time / Intentional Flexibility constraint]** is key to making this plan realistic because **coding and econometric modeling tasks often encounter unexpected bugs or data errors that delay the schedule**."

## Lab 2-3: Structured Prompt Design
Çalışma koçu talimatları hiyerarşik bir ana şablon (Master Template) haline getirildi.
* **Activity Reflection:** "I intentionally designed the **[Constraints Section]** to **limit the heavy analytical study blocks to a maximum of 3 hours a day** because **preventing mental fatigue is crucial when dealing with complex mathematical proofs and algorithms**."
* "The reason I chose **[Specific Rule: Mandatory Pomodoro Breaks]** for the **[Instructions Section]** was to prevent the AI from **generating a continuous, unbroken 5-hour study block that is impossible for a human to execute**."

## Lab 2-4: Model Execution and 1st Result Check
Oluşturulan yapılandırılmış komut test edildi ve "Gerçeklik Kuralları"na göre değerlendirildi.
* **Activity Reflection:** "Initially, the AI **[Behavior/Mistake: packed too many intensive topics into a single day without accounting for review time]**, so I plan to refine the **[Negative Prompt / Constraints Section]** by **explicitly forbidding more than two new statistical concepts per day** to improve accuracy."

## Lab 2-5: Prompt 1st Tuning and Re-execution
İlk çalıştırmadaki hatalar ve komut sapmaları (instruction drift) düzeltilerek şablon iyileştirildi.
* **Activity Reflection:** "By adjusting the **[Verification / Output Logic Section]**, I was able to solve the problem of **unrealistic daily task loads**, resulting in a plan that is **highly flexible, automatically shifts uncompleted tasks to the weekend, and is easy to adhere to during intensive study periods**."
